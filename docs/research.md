# Research workflow

WHOIZE treats the CAPTCHA as a measurable perception experiment.

## Human baseline

Record success rate, response time, click error, device class, and selected
parameter profile. Do not collect unnecessary identity data.

## Solver baseline

Compare at least:

- a single frozen frame;
- short frame stacks;
- frame differencing;
- temporal averaging;
- optical flow and coherent-particle clustering.

## Reporting

Publish parameter sets, sample sizes, solver assumptions, and failure cases.
Avoid claims of bot resistance without a reproducible attack benchmark and a
human usability baseline.
